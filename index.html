The platform

Six things it does,
properly.

Not a CRM with a progression tab bolted on. A system built for one job by people who do that job every day.

01

Workflow

Every sale carries its own lifecycle, its own chain status, its own enquiry and searches state. Boards can hold your own fields and your own option lists, so you're not forced into someone else's process. Auction stock runs on its own lifecycle alongside private treaty.

02

Communication

Write your own templates once and send them in your own branding. Optional milestone updates keep vendors and buyers informed at exchange, completion and the moments in between — switched on per board, never by default.

03

Visibility

Every email is logged with what actually happened to it — delivered, bounced, auto-replied. "I emailed the solicitor" stops being a claim and becomes a record with a date on it.

04

SPX AI

Reads the case history and tells you where the sale really is. Drafts the update email in the tone you choose. Pulls the details out of a Memorandum of Sale so nobody retypes them.

05

Client experience

Vendors and buyers stop ringing to ask what's happening, because they already know. The updates go out in your name, in your branding, on the milestones that matter to them.

06

Reporting & analytics

Pipeline distribution, aged pipeline, time to exchange, fallthrough rate, traffic-light status, capacity and holiday cover — on desktop, and on a reporting view actually designed for a phone.
