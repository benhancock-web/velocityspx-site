The case file

Everything in one place, every update, time-stamped, categorised, and recorded

Open a case and everything you need is there. No second system, no shared inbox, no substandard system to try and bend into shape.

Status, tenure, buyer type, chain status and owner at the top. Then key dates, progression milestones, legal and conveyancing, mortgage and survey.

  • Every date the sale turns on, in one column
  • Milestones tick off as the case moves
  • Change a status and the board updates instantly

The full history, categorised

Every call, email, solicitor reply, survey result and chain change, tagged and dated against the case.

  • Categorised, so you can filter to just the solicitor thread

The whole chain view, with readiness indicators

Each link with its agent, its solicitors and whether it's ready to exchange — and your sale highlighted in the middle of it.

Everyone involved, one tap away

Vendor, buyer, both solicitors, the broker and the surveyor, with direct numbers and email addresses.

Questions

The things agencies ask first

Does this replace or complement our CRM?

No, and it isn't trying to. Reapit, Alto, Street and Dezrez win the instruction, market the property and agree the sale. VelocitySPX takes over at the memorandum of sale and runs the case to completion. Your negotiators carry on exactly as they are.

How long does it take to get started?

A first-run setup wizard walks you through your boards, your fields, your statuses and your email templates. Most of the configuration is choosing how your team already works rather than learning ours.

What does it cost?

Start with three months free of charge, with no credit card. After that, pricing is per progressor, per month, with no setup fee — and we'll give you a straight number on the demo call once we know how many people you'd put on it. If the value isn't clear, you walk away and export your data.

Will our clients know we’re using it?

Only if you tell them. Every email, update and document goes out in your agency's name, from your address, with your branding. VelocitySPX never appears in front of a vendor or a buyer.

Is our data secure, and is it kept separate from other agencies?

Yes. Each agency's data is isolated at the database level, not just hidden in the interface, and the platform is UK-hosted. We act as your data processor under a written agreement.

How will VelocitySPX fit into the way our team already works?

It sits on top of how you work now rather than replacing it. Your negotiators carry on in the CRM, the same people keep progressing the same cases, and VelocitySPX picks each sale up once it's agreed. Your boards, statuses, fields and email templates are set to match your process during onboarding, and we walk your team through it rather than handing over a login and a manual.

Can you also provide expert sales progression cover when we're short-staffed or someone is away?

Momentum Sales Progression, the business VelocitySPX was built inside, can pick up a caseload inside the same platform when you're a person down — holiday, sickness or a spike in volume — and hand it straight back. It's optional, and no software vendor can offer it, because no software vendor does the work.
