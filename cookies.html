What we use

We use one analytics tool, Google Analytics, to understand how visitors find and use this site, for example which pages are viewed and roughly how people arrived here. We do not use cookies for advertising, and we do not use any other tracking on this site.

Google Analytics only runs if you accept it, either in the banner shown on your first visit or using the buttons above. If you reject it, or simply close the banner without choosing, it stays off.

What it sets

NamePurposeDuration
vspx_analytics_consentRemembers your accept/reject choice on this site. Not itself used for tracking.Stored on your device until you clear it

We turn on IP anonymisation in Google Analytics, so your full IP address is not stored against your visit.

Changing your mind

Use the buttons above at any time to switch analytics on or off. You can also clear cookies for this site in your browser settings, which will show you the banner again on your next visit.

Who processes this data

Google Analytics is provided by Google, based in the United States. See our Privacy Notice for how we handle personal data more generally, including international transfers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “last updated” date at the top of the page.
